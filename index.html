DISTO X3 Test version 5.9 (phone app with camera)

Connects to the Leica DISTO over Bluetooth, records readings and works out the spread. Open this page in Google Chrome on an Android phone, or in Chrome or Edge on a computer with Bluetooth. iPhones cannot use it (use the Leica DISTO Plan app instead).

1. Connect

Turn the DISTO on and switch its Bluetooth on (in its settings menu). Close the Leica phone app if it is open, because only one thing can connect at a time.

2. Take readings

If the buttons here do nothing, press the red button on the DISTO itself. Readings taken that way are still recorded here.

Camera (films while the readings run)

Uses the phone's back camera. Photos and videos are saved to the phone's Downloads folder. Keep each video under about 2 minutes.

5-minute test

Connect the DISTO first, aim it at the post, then press the button and step away. For 5 minutes it takes a reading about every 2 to 4 seconds, a set of photos every 30 seconds (at three brightness levels if the phone allows) and a 30 second video at the start. It beeps once when it starts and five times when it is done (turn the media volume up). At the end everything is saved as ONE zip file in Downloads, to send to Claude. Fill in the tape distance, target and conditions in section 3 before starting.

3. Save this set

Measure the tape distance from the same point on the DISTO that it measures from. By default that is its back end.
