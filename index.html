DISTO X3 Test version 5.2 (phone app)

Connects to the Leica DISTO over Bluetooth, records readings and works out the spread. Open this page in Google Chrome on an Android phone, or in Chrome or Edge on a computer with Bluetooth. iPhones cannot use it (use the Leica DISTO Plan app instead).

1. Connect

Turn the DISTO on and switch its Bluetooth on (in its settings menu). Close the Leica phone app if it is open, because only one thing can connect at a time.

Not connected

2. Take readings

If the buttons here do nothing, press the red button on the DISTO itself. Readings taken that way are still recorded here.

Tries the Leica X3 command first, then a few others, and stops at the first one that makes the DISTO measure.
